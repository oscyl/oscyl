Third-party libraries in this download
======================================

Oscyl itself is licensed MIT OR Apache-2.0 (LICENSE-MIT, LICENSE-APACHE).
The Rust crates built into it are listed with their licences in the app's
About window.

To run without anything else installed, this download also carries GTK,
libadwaita, GLib, Pango, Cairo, HarfBuzz and the libraries they use,
unmodified, as separate shared libraries (DLLs on Windows, dylibs on macOS),
and the Adwaita icon theme. GTK, libadwaita, GLib and Pango are licensed under
the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later; the others under
the licences whose texts are in this folder.

PACKAGES.txt names the exact build of each one: the packages of MSYS2
(https://packages.msys2.org) on Windows, the formulae of Homebrew
(https://formulae.brew.sh) on macOS. Their sources at those versions are
published there. You may replace any of these libraries with your own build
of a compatible version; Oscyl loads them from this folder.
